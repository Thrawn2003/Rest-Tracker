        Energy forecast for today

          Caffeine in your body

            Slow breathing

            Longer, slower exhales calm the body. Try five minutes in bed with the lights low. Stop if you feel dizzy and go back to normal breathing.

            Ready when you are

            Lying awake?

            1. Don't check the clock. It only adds pressure.
            2. If it feels like 20 minutes or more, get up. Keep lights low and sit somewhere comfortable.
            3. Do something dull and calm: read a paper book, stretch gently, or listen to quiet audio.
            4. Go back to bed when your eyes get heavy, not at a set time.
            5. Keep your wake-up time the same tomorrow, even after a bad night. That is what repairs sleep.

            Poor sleep that lasts three months or more, loud snoring with gasping, or sleepiness that makes driving unsafe are reasons to see a doctor. Good treatment exists, including CBT-I, a structured program that works better than sleeping pills over the long run.
